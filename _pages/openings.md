# Open Positions

We are always interested in hearing from motivated researchers who share our passion for social psychology, intergroup relations, and social justice.

### PhD Students
If you are interested in pursuing a PhD in our group, please get in touch with details of your research interests and a CV. We particularly welcome applicants with a background in social psychology, political psychology, or related fields. Funding opportunities through the Spanish Ministry and the Basque Government are available periodically — contact us to find out about current calls.

### Postdoctoral Researchers
We welcome postdoctoral researchers, especially those with external fellowships. Relevant schemes include:
- [Juan de la Cierva](https://www.aei.gob.es/convocatorias/buscador-convocatorias/ayudas-contratos-juan-de-la-cierva-formacion-2023) (Spanish Ministry)
- [Marie Skłodowska-Curie Individual Fellowships](https://marie-sklodowska-curie-actions.ec.europa.eu/actions/postdoctoral-fellowships) (EU)
- [Ikerbasque](https://www.ikerbasque.net/en) Research Fellowships (Basque Government)

We are happy to support strong candidates in developing fellowship applications.

### Master & Bachelor Students
We occasionally supervise master's and bachelor's theses. If you are a student at UPV/EHU and interested in working with us, please reach out to any group member.
